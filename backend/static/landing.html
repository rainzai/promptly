How it works

Pick a subject, add your material, learn in order.

No generic course, no dead-end question dumps. Promptly builds each step around your lecture and your goal.

  1. Choose your topic

    Bayesian statistics, Foucault and biopower, Python pandas — or bring your own.

  2. Add study material

    Upload slides, a PDF or your notes, then optionally set a goal: “prepare for Week 3 lecture”, “understand this paper”, “prepare for exam”.

  3. Get your prerequisite map

    A visual path from where you are to where you are going — no overwhelming wall of topics.

  4. Start exactly where it fits

    A 3–5 question check estimates your starting level. You can always override it.

Features

Everything you need to actually learn it.

Built around a simple loop: see where you are, train, prove it, and keep the streak alive.

Quick placement check

  • 3–5 questions sampling different levels
  • We estimate where you should begin
  • You can always override and jump ahead

Bootcamp

  • Concepts to understand per level
  • A tiny explanation or source reference
  • 2–4 questions with optional hints
  • “I already know this” — skip it

Mastery checkpoint

  • A harder question that connects several concepts
  • Pass to unlock the next stage
  • Feedback tied to your study goal

Progress + social

  • XP and a mastery score
  • A private friend leaderboard
  • Streaks and completion milestones
  • Subject-specific badges
